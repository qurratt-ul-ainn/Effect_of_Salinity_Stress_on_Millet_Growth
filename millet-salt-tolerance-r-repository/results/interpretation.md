# Results interpretation

## Descriptive pattern

The supplied experiment contains five salinity levels with three replications per treatment. The descriptive analysis shows a progressive decline in millet growth and biomass as salinity increases.

For total dry biomass, the observed treatment means were:

- 1 dS m⁻¹: 5.64 g
- 4 dS m⁻¹: 5.03 g
- 8 dS m⁻¹: 4.71 g
- 12 dS m⁻¹: 3.54 g
- 16 dS m⁻¹: 2.85 g

For plant height, the observed treatment means were:

- 1 dS m⁻¹: 42.83 cm
- 4 dS m⁻¹: 36.90 cm
- 8 dS m⁻¹: 31.37 cm
- 12 dS m⁻¹: 25.57 cm
- 16 dS m⁻¹: 21.17 cm

## Statistical interpretation

The ANOVA and Tukey outputs should be used to determine which treatment differences are statistically supported. Descriptive declines should not be described as statistically significant unless the inferential tests support that conclusion.

## Biological interpretation

A consistent decline in height, root development, and biomass with increasing salinity would be consistent with the general physiological burden imposed by salt stress. The repository deliberately separates descriptive patterns from statistical inference.

## Reproducibility

All values in this document are derived from the supplied dataset. Re-run the numbered scripts after any data correction before using the interpretation in a manuscript.
